About Us

Aldebaran Trading

Aldebaran Trading W.L.L is a premier automotive spare parts supplier based in the Industrial Area of Al-Rayyan, Doha, Qatar. We specialize in sourcing and delivering genuine and OEM-grade parts for a wide spectrum of vehicles — from German luxury brands to American muscle, Japanese reliability, and Korean value.

One of our key strengths is the ability to provide parts at the shortest possible lead time, without compromising on quality, price competitiveness, or service standards.

  • Ford, GMC & Chevrolet Parts
  • Toyota, Nissan & Honda Parts
  • Audi, Volkswagen & Volvo Parts
  • Land Rover & Jaguar Parts
  • KIA, Hyundai & More
